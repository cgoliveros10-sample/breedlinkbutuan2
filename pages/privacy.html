Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. Here's exactly what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Effective: November 15, 2026 Version 1.1

01

Information We Collect

When you create a BreedLink account or interact with our platform, we collect certain information to deliver and improve our services. This falls into two broad categories: information you provide directly, and information collected automatically.

Information you provide:

  • Account details such as your full name, username, email address, password, and chosen account type (Breeder, Veterinarian, or Pet Lover)
  • Profile information including your location, profile photo, cover photo, bio, and the contact details you choose to show (email, phone, location)
  • Animal listings: name, breed, type, age, gender, status, description, preferred clinic, photos, and the vaccination and health documents you upload
  • Content you create: posts, comments, messages, and media uploads
  • Your activity on the platform, such as animals you like or pass on, people you follow, posts you like or save, and ratings and reviews you give
  • Vet verification requests you submit, and the decision and reason a veterinarian gives
  • For veterinarians: your clinic name and licence number, used to review your vet application
  • Communications you send to BreedLink or other users through the platform

Information collected automatically:

  • Browser and device information (user agent) saved with some activity, such as swiping on an animal
  • The animal and direction (like or pass) of each swipe
  • IP address and standard request logs kept by our hosting and database providers

We only collect what we genuinely need to operate BreedLink. You are always in control of what profile information you choose to share publicly.

02

How We Use Your Data

Your data helps us provide a safe, personalized experience for the BreedLink community. We use it to:

  • Create and maintain your account, verify your email with a one-time code, and keep your account secure
  • Match you with breeders and pet owners based on your swipes, filters, and location
  • Display your public profile, animal listings, and posts to other community members
  • Let approved veterinarians review verification requests you submit, and show a Vet-Verified badge on approved listings and posts
  • Send transactional emails such as verification codes, password resets, and important platform notices
  • Diagnose technical problems and keep the platform working
  • Detect and prevent fraud, spam, and activity that violates our Terms and Conditions
  • Comply with legal obligations applicable to our platform

We do not use your data to train third-party AI models, and we do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

03

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it in the following limited circumstances:

  • With other users — information you choose to make public (name, profile photo, bio, location, animal listings, posts, and ratings you give) is visible to registered BreedLink members
  • Uploaded files — photos and documents are stored with a file link, so anyone who has the link can open the file. Avoid uploading documents that contain sensitive personal details
  • With approved veterinarians and administrators — when you request vet verification, the animal or post details and the documents you attached are shown to the vets and administrators who review it
  • With service providers — Supabase (database, login, and file storage), Vercel (website hosting), Brevo (sending verification code emails), and Google Fonts (page fonts, which are loaded from Google). They process data on our behalf
  • For legal compliance — when required by applicable law, court order, or government request, or to protect the safety of our users or the public
  • With your explicit consent — for any other purpose not listed above, we will ask for your permission first
04

Cookies & Tracking

BreedLink does not set its own cookies. To keep you signed in and make pages load faster, it uses your browser's storage:

  • Session storage — keeps you signed in while your tab is open and holds your sign-in token and profile details
  • Local storage — stores a refresh token so your session can continue, your email or username if you tick "Remember me", and small settings such as conversations you hid
  • Third-party services — our hosting provider and Google Fonts may record technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads pages and fonts

You can clear this storage in your browser settings, and you will be signed out. We do not currently use analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking.

05

Data Security

We use the following safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Data sent between your browser and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS
  • Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase), which stores them hashed; we never see or store your plaintext password
  • New accounts must verify their email with a one-time code
  • Database access rules limit what each user can read and change
  • Access to the database dashboard is limited to the BreedLink development team
  • If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify affected users as required by the Data Privacy Act

No system is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your BreedLink account.

06

Data Retention

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. Specifically:

  • Active account data is kept for the lifetime of your account
  • When you delete your account from Account Settings, it is scheduled for deletion. You have 7 days to recover it. After that, your account and personal data are permanently deleted, except where the law requires us to keep something
  • Deleting a conversation only hides it from your view. The other person keeps their copy of the messages
  • Backup copies kept by our database provider may take additional time to be purged

You can delete your account at any time from Account Settings. You can also contact us to request deletion of specific data (see Section 7).

07

Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173), you have the right to:

  • Be informed — know what personal data we collect and how we use it, as described in this policy
  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete information, which you can do yourself by editing your profile and Account Settings
  • Erasure or blocking — request that we delete your account and associated personal data, or stop using it
  • Data portability — receive your data in a commonly used format so you can take it elsewhere
  • Object — object to processing of your data based on our legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — at any time for any processing based solely on your consent
  • Complain and seek damages — file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) if you believe your data was mishandled

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@breedlink.com. We will respond within 30 days. You will not be charged for exercising your rights, and we will not discriminate against you for doing so.

08

Children's Privacy

BreedLink is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at privacy@breedlink.com and we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

If we discover that we have inadvertently collected data from a user under 18 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete that data as soon as possible.

09

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, our features, or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Effective" date and version at the top of this page
  • Notify you by email or with a prominent notice on BreedLink
  • Show a notice when you next sign in

Your continued use of BreedLink after the effective date of any updated policy constitutes your acceptance of the changes. If you do not agree, you may delete your account before the changes take effect.
